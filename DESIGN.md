# J.A.R.V.I.S. / Hermes Field Guide

## Surface
A **Decide / Learn** manual with secondary **Command / Inspect** navigation. This is not a live agent dashboard. No fake telemetry, operational agents, or connectivity indicators.

## Reference translation
Use the user's jarvis-hud vocabulary: midnight #030a12, cyan #4fd8ff, precise angular corners, Rajdhani display type and Share Tech Mono instrument labels. Replace tiny all-caps HUD body text with Manrope at 16–17px / 1.75. No scanlines over reading content, boot delays, fake clocks, glassmorphism or audio autoplay. The arc motif illustrates the reasoning/action/verification loop; it doesn't pretend to show runtime data.

## Layout
Persistent left chapter nav, single-line context/search/actions header, generous constrained reading column, light on-page contents rail at wide widths. Overview leads with editorial typography and one restrained arc graphic. Final chapters: JARVIS then startup team. Mobile: persistent icon+label bottom nav; chapters and preferences in HeroUI Pro sheets.

## System
HeroUI v3 OSS and Pro share root semantic tokens (background, surface, foreground, muted, accent, borders, focus, overlay). Portals inherit the root theme. Solid surfaces, hairline structural borders, small radii. Cyan for navigation/action; amber only for genuine caveats. No nested cards. Self-hosted fonts. Dark HUD and a fully usable paper/light theme. Comfortable and compact reading.

## Accessibility and evidence
44px touch targets, semantic landmarks, skip link, focus-visible, labelled icon controls, reduced motion, user-controlled progress stored locally. No global overflow hiding. Long code/table scroll locally. Search indexes real full chapter content. Source review date and provenance visible. Test 320,375,390,768,1024,1280 and 1536 widths and real browser interactions before publication.
